The Math Behind the Magic

AttendSmartly uses precise formulas to compute your Safe Bunks and Recovery Classes. Cancelled classes are automatically excluded from conducted totals so your percentage remains perfectly accurate.

Safe Bunks Formula:
Floor( (Present - (Target * Total)) / Target )
